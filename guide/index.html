Remote guide

Step by step: what to press, what you'll see.

START HEREGet the phone remote

Your phone is the remote for everything the TV remote can't do: alerts, channels, leagues, settings.

  1. On your TV remote, press ▼ Down.
  2. The game list drops down on the left of the screen. The QR code is at the bottom of it, next to "Scan: your phone is the remote".
  3. Point your phone's camera at the QR code and tap the link that pops up. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. No app to install.
  4. Press Back to close the game list.

Tip: add the page to your phone's home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen). After the first scan, fantasytvapp.com/remote opens your TV's remote too.

WITH THE TV REMOTE

Turn AutoTune off (stay on this game)

  1. Press ▲ Up. The red tag at the bottom left changes to AUTOTUNE OFF: the TV stays where it is.
  2. Press ▲ again to turn AutoTune back on.

Stop a switch

  1. A 5-second countdown shows before every switch.
  2. Press OK or Back to stay put.

Rewind

  1. ◀ Left: back 10 seconds (again for more, up to a minute).
  2. ▶ Right: back to live.

Watch a different game

  1. Press ▼ Down to open the game list.
  2. Press ▲ ▼ to highlight the game, then OK to watch it.
  3. It stays on for 45 seconds, then AutoTune picks again. To keep it on, press ▲ Up to turn AutoTune off.

Heart a game (AutoTune favors it)

  1. Press ▼ Down to open the game list.
  2. Press ▲ ▼ to highlight the game.
  3. Press ▶ Right, or OK twice quickly: the ♥ on that game fills in. Do it again to remove it.

Hearted games win close calls, and AutoTune goes to them first during commercials.

Hide the scores and graphics

  1. Press ▼ Down, then ▲ Up to the top row, On screen.
  2. Press OK to switch: Full (everything) → Scores only (just your leagues' scores) → Clean (nothing but the game; AutoTune stays put).

Popups: watch the play

  1. When a popup slides in at the bottom right, press OK to watch that game. Several up? ▲ ▼ pick one.

ON THE PHONE REMOTE

Change your alerts

  1. Tap the ⚙ gear (top right).
  2. Tap ALERTS, pick Quiet, More or Everything (or Custom).
  3. Tap SAVE TO TV.

Your IPTV / channels

  1. Tap ⚙, then CHANNELS.
  2. Enter your login (Dispatcharr, Xtream or Stalker), tap TEST CONNECTION.
  3. Tap SAVE TO TV.

Your leagues

  1. Tap ⚙, then LEAGUES.
  2. Add Sleeper, ESPN or Yahoo, tap SAVE TO TV.

Scores early or late?

  1. Tap ⚙, then WATCH.
  2. Under Stream delay, type the game clock you see on TV, tap SYNC.

On each game's card

Tap ♥ to heart it · the shield to lock it (stay on it) · ⊘ to skip it (never tune) · WATCH ON TV to put it on now. At the top: the AutoTune switch, ON SCREEN (Full · Scores only · Clean), rewind, and WATCH NFL REDZONE.
