ON THE TV REMOTE

▲

AutoTune on / off

AutoTune switches to the game that matters most to your lineup. Press Up to stay on this game; Up again turns it back on. Before a switch, a 5‑second countdown shows: OK or Back cancels it.

▼

Every game

All the live games, yours first. Up/Down move, OK watches one, Back closes. ▶ hearts a game: AutoTune favors it in close calls and goes to it first during breaks.

A game you pick stays on for 45 seconds; to keep it, turn AutoTune off with ▲ (or Lock it on your phone).

OK

Hide the overlays

The On screen row at the top of that list switches what sits over the game:

Fulleverything
Scores onlyyour leagues' scores, small popups
Cleanjust the game, AutoTune stays put
◀ ▶

Rewind · back to live

Left jumps back 10 seconds (again for more, up to a minute) in the game you're watching. Right goes back to live.

OK

YOUR PHONE IS THE REMOTE

QR

Press ▼ Down on your TV remote

The game list opens, and the QR code is at the bottom of it. Scan it with your phone's camera: same Wi-Fi as the TV, no app to install. After that, fantasytvapp.com/remote opens your TV's remote straight away.

Add the page to your home screen to keep it one tap away.
