RedZone for your fantasy teams.

FantasyTV watches every live NFL game for you and switches your TV to the one your players are about to score in, on your own IPTV channels, with your live fantasy scores on screen. Nothing is spoiled: everything waits for your stream.

What it does

One screen for your whole fantasy Sunday.

  • AutoTuneRanks every live drive by your starters on the field, field position and down, and switches to the one that matters most, with a countdown you can cancel.
  • Catches big plays before they airESPN reports plays before your stream shows them. When one of your players breaks a big one in another game, the TV gets there in time to watch it.
  • Never sits on a commercialDuring a break it switches to a game that's actually playing, and goes back when yours resumes.
  • Your players on screenCards for your players in the game you're watching, with live points and headshots, plus your matchups and live win chances.
  • Touchdown momentsYour player scores and his photo pops out of his card. Touchdowns in other games wait until they air, so the moment is never spoiled.
  • Spoiler-safeScores, popups and switches all follow your stream's delay, never ahead of it. Sync it once by typing the game clock you see.
  • Split screenTwo of your drives hot at once? The game on screen moves left, the other plays muted on the right.
  • Your phone is the remoteSettings, the channel for each game, rewind and alerts, all from your phone on the same Wi-Fi.

Install

About two minutes, on the TV itself.

  1. Get Downloader on your TVInstall Downloader (by AFTVnews) from your TV's app store. Works on Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV running Android 8 or newer: NVIDIA Shield, onn 4K, Chromecast with Google TV and most Google TVs.
  2. Enter the codeIn Downloader, type 1790534 and install. It's always the newest version, and FantasyTV updates itself after that.
  3. Scan the code on your TVOpen FantasyTV and scan its QR code with your phone's camera (same Wi-Fi). Add your Sleeper username or ESPN league and your IPTV login, then Save to TV.

What you need

  • Fantasy leaguesSleeper (just your username) and/or ESPN (league ID; private leagues work too). Up to four leagues at once. Yahoo is coming.
  • IPTVAn Xtream Codes login (the one TiviMate uses) or a Dispatcharr server. FantasyTV finds each game on your channels, and you can pick any game's channel yourself.
  • An Android TV boxPowerful boxes like the NVIDIA Shield get every feature. Lower-powered sticks run a smoother, lighter mode automatically.

Questions

Where do my logins go?

Only onto your own TV. There's no FantasyTV account and no FantasyTV server: the app talks to your IPTV provider, ESPN, Sleeper and GitHub (for updates), and nothing else.

How does fantasytvapp.com/remote find my TV?

The QR code on your TV includes its address on your home Wi-Fi. Your phone keeps it, and the website never sees it (it's after the # in the link, which browsers don't send). Next time, fantasytvapp.com/remote opens your remote straight away.

Can I rewind?

Yes, the game you're watching: ◀ on the TV remote goes back 10 seconds, up to a minute, from when you tuned in. Other games can't be rewound; switching to one starts it live.

Does it include channels?

No. FantasyTV plays your own IPTV subscription; it doesn't provide streams.

Can I try it without a game on?

Yes: Practice Sunday in the phone's settings runs simulated games any day of the week.
