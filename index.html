RedZone for your fantasy teams.

Regular RedZone chases the league's best plays. FantasyTV chases yours.

FantasyTV watches every live NFL game for you and switches your TV (or your phone) to the one your players are about to score in, on your own IPTV channels, with your live fantasy scores on screen. Nothing is spoiled: everything waits for your stream.

What it does

One screen for your whole fantasy Sunday.

  • AutoTuneRanks every live drive by your starters on the field, field position and down, and switches to the one that matters most, with a countdown you can cancel.
  • Catches big plays before they airESPN reports plays before your stream shows them. When one of your players breaks a big one in another game, the TV gets there in time to watch it.
  • Never sits on a commercialDuring a break it switches to a game that's actually playing, and goes back when yours resumes.
  • Your players on screenCards for your players in the game you're watching, with live points and headshots, plus your matchups and live win chances.
  • Touchdown momentsYour player scores and his photo pops out of his card. Touchdowns in other games wait until they air, so the moment is never spoiled.
  • Spoiler-safeScores, popups and switches all follow your stream's delay, never ahead of it. Sync it once by typing the game clock you see.
  • Split screenTwo of your drives hot at once? The game on screen moves left, the other plays muted on the right.
  • Your phone is the remoteSettings, the channel for each game, rewind and alerts, all from your phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Or watch on your phoneThe same app on an Android phone: the game on top with your players and scores below, full screen sideways, and picture-in-picture when you switch apps. Tap a popup to watch that game, swipe to rewind.
  • Made for the Galaxy FoldFolded, unfolded or half-open like a laptop: the layout follows the screen without restarting the stream.

Install

One app for your TV and your phone: the same download, and it sets itself up for the screen it's on. About two minutes.

On a TV Android TV · Google TV · Fire TV

  1. Get Downloader on your TVInstall Downloader (by AFTVnews) from your TV's app store. Works on Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV running Android 8 or newer: NVIDIA Shield, onn 4K, Chromecast with Google TV and most Google TVs.
  2. Enter the codeIn Downloader, type 1790534 and install. It's always the newest version, and FantasyTV updates itself after that.
  3. Scan the code on your TVOpen FantasyTV and scan its QR code with your phone's camera (same Wi-Fi). The phone walks you through it: first your stream server (your IPTV login, since FantasyTV plays the games from your service), then your Sleeper or ESPN teams. Save to TV and you're watching.

On a phone Android phones · Galaxy Fold and other foldables

  1. Download itOn the phone, tap Download for Android or open aftv.news/1790534 in its browser (the same code as the TV; it starts the download after a few seconds). It's always the newest version.
  2. Allow the installAndroid asks once to allow installs from your browser: allow it, then tap Install. FantasyTV updates itself after that.
  3. Set it up on the phoneOpen FantasyTV: it walks you through it right there, your stream server first, then your Sleeper or ESPN teams. No QR code, no second phone: the phone is the remote.

Questions

Which download is for my TV, and which for my phone?

They're the same: one app, one code (1790534). It sees what it's running on and sets itself up for it: on a TV, your phone becomes the remote; on a phone, touch controls and layouts for the phone, folded or not.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. FantasyTV is Android only: Android TV, Google TV and Android phones and tablets. An iPhone still works as the remote for a FantasyTV TV, in its browser.

Where do my logins go?

Only onto your own TV or phone. There's no FantasyTV account and no FantasyTV server: the app talks to your IPTV provider, ESPN, Sleeper and GitHub (for updates), and nothing else. On a phone, its settings can't be reached from the Wi-Fi it's on, not even at a coffee shop.

How does fantasytvapp.com/remote find my TV?

The QR code on your TV includes its address on your home Wi-Fi. Your phone keeps it, and the website never sees it (it's after the # in the link, which browsers don't send). Next time, fantasytvapp.com/remote opens your remote straight away.

Can I rewind?

Yes, the game you're watching: ◀ on the TV remote (or a swipe left on a phone) goes back 10 seconds, up to a minute, from when you tuned in. Other games can't be rewound; switching to one starts it live.

Does it include channels?

No. FantasyTV plays your own IPTV subscription; it doesn't provide streams.

Can I try it without a game on?

Yes: Practice Sunday in Settings (Watch) runs simulated games any day of the week.
